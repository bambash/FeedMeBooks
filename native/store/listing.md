Text for the Play Console store listing. Limits are Play's.

**App name** (30 characters max)

FeedMeBooks

**Short description** (80 characters max)

Read the ebook, hear the audiobook, and switch between them without losing your place.

**Full description** (4,000 characters max)

Read it. Hear it. Never lose your place.

FeedMeBooks pairs an ebook with its audiobook. Read on the bus, press play when you get
off, and the narrator picks up at the paragraph you were on. Stop listening, open the book,
and it lands on the sentence the narrator just read.

HOW IT FINDS YOUR PLACE
The app listens to a few seconds of the audiobook on your phone and matches the words
against the book's text. There is no index to build and nothing is sent anywhere: speech
recognition runs on the device, and the more you switch, the sharper it gets.

YOUR OWN BOOKS
Add any DRM-free EPUB and the folder of audio files that goes with it (MP3, M4A, M4B, MP4
and AAC). Files are picked with Android's file picker; the app keeps a read-only link to
your audio and never uploads anything.

READING
• Paged or scrolling layout, with auto-scroll
• Light, sepia and dark themes; adjustable text size
• Table of contents
• Follow the narrator: while the audio plays, the sentence being read is highlighted and
  the pages turn by themselves

LISTENING
• Background playback with lock-screen and notification controls
• Skip back and forward 30 seconds, playback speed
• Sleep timer that fades out
• Select any passage and play from there

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no analytics, no ads. Everything stays on your phone. The only download is the
75 MB speech model, once.

FeedMeBooks needs a 64-bit Android phone running Android 8.0 or later. It works with
DRM-free books and audio you already own; it does not sell or stream books.
